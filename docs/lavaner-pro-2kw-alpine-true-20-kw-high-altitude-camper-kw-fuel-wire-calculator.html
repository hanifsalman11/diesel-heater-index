Frequently Asked Questions (Lavaner Pro 2kW Alpine (True 2.0 kW) High-Altitude Camper)

Should I buy a 2kW or 5kW/8kW diesel heater for Lavaner Pro 2kW Alpine (True 2.0 kW) High-Altitude Camper?

For spaces up to 320 cu. ft. (Sprinter 144, Transit 148, truck campers, roof-top tents), a 2kW heater (6,824 BTU/hr, 60mm duct) is far superior because it runs at medium-high output where combustion stays above 550°C and burns off carbon soot. Use a 5kW ('8kW' marketed, 16,300+ BTU/hr, 75mm duct) only for spaces over 400 cu. ft., uninsulated ice shanties, or garages.

How much diesel fuel and 12V battery Ah does Lavaner Pro 2kW Alpine (True 2.0 kW) High-Altitude Camper use per night?

Burning 0.11 L/hr on Low and 0.26 L/hr on High (~0.17 L/hr average), a 10-hour winter night uses 1.7 Liters (0.45 gallons), giving ~58.8 hours on a 10-Liter tank. Electrically, it uses ~19.4 Ah per 10-hour night (including the 13.5A glow-plug start/stop cycles and 1.8A fan run).

What wire gauge do I need to stop E-01 undervoltage errors on a 12V diesel heater?

During the 3-minute glow-plug ignition and shutdown burn-off, current spikes to 13.5 Amps. To keep voltage at the heater's internal ECU above the 10.8V low-voltage cutoff, cut off the thin factory harness and run 10 AWG Marine Tinned Copper (15A–20A Blade Fuse) for runs up to 12 feet (or 8 AWG for 15–25 feet).
